From MLT Graduate to Running a Growth Agency — The Cre8Labs Story

🎯

I didn't start out as a marketer.

I studied Medical Lab Technology (MLT). My job was running diagnostic tests, analyzing blood samples, and looking at reports all day. A world of precision, protocols, and science. Marketing? That was as far from my reality as you could imagine.

But here's the thing about working in healthcare — you see the gap. You see how many service-based businesses (clinics, labs, healthcare providers) struggle not because their services aren't good, but because nobody knows they exist.

And that's where it all started.

The Turning Point

I realized that the same analytical mindset I used in the lab — collecting data, identifying patterns, testing hypotheses — could be applied to marketing. The difference was: instead of diagnosing diseases, I was diagnosing funnels. Instead of running tests on samples, I was running ad campaigns.

I started small. Read everything I could about copywriting, paid ads, and conversion optimization. Tested strategies on my own projects. Failed. Learned. Tested again.

"The lab taught me precision. Marketing taught me psychology. Combining both changed everything."

The First Breakthrough

My first real client was a frozen food brand based in Mansehra — Frozen Dastarkhan. They had zero online presence. No social footprint, no website, no ad infrastructure. Everything had to be built from scratch.

We launched a Facebook Ads campaign. Within 45 days, we had generated 411 leads at just Rs 26.67 per lead and delivered over 150 paying customers.

That's when I knew: this wasn't just a side project. This was a real business that could help real people grow.

From One Client to 50+

Word spread. Soon came Onezon Store — a Shopify e-commerce brand. We rebuilt their store, overhauled their ad strategy, and generated Rs 265,180 in sales with 128 orders in the first month.

Then came healthcare practices, e-commerce brands, local businesses, coaches, and consultants. Each one with a unique challenge. Each one solved using the same precision-based approach: audit, architect, launch, optimize.

Today, Cre8Labs has worked with 50+ businesses across multiple industries — from frozen food to e-commerce, from healthcare to coaching.

The Cre8Labs Philosophy

My MLT background shaped everything about how we work:

  • Data First: Just like a lab report, we don't make decisions without evidence. Every campaign is built on data.
  • System Over Hacks: We don't believe in "growth hacks." We build repeatable systems that produce consistent results.
  • Revenue > Vanity: We optimize for one thing — revenue growth. Not likes, not followers, not traffic that doesn't convert.
"An MLT graduate running ad campaigns sounds unlikely. But the truth is, the skills that make a good medical technologist — precision, analysis, attention to detail — are exactly the skills that make a great marketer."

What's Next?

We're not stopping here. Our goal is to help 100+ businesses build predictable, scalable growth systems. We're expanding our services, refining our frameworks, and continuing to prove that you don't need a marketing degree to build a marketing agency — you just need the willingness to learn, test, and never settle.

If you're a service-based business tired of wasting money on marketing that doesn't work, let's talk. We'll audit your funnel for free and show you exactly where the growth opportunities are.
