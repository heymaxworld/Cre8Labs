Case Study

Onezon Store — Rs 265K Sales in Month 1

How a Shopify store rebuild and Facebook Ads campaign turned a struggling e-commerce brand into a revenue machine.

OZ
Rs 265K Total Sales (Month 1)
128 Orders Placed
4,810+ Website Visitors

The Client

Onezon Store is a Shopify-based e-commerce brand based in Pakistan. They had a product people wanted, but were struggling with wrong audience targeting, low ad budget, and poor lead handling. Their store wasn't converting — and they didn't know why.

The Challenge

Onezon Store was new to e-commerce marketing. Their ad campaigns were targeting the wrong audiences, their Shopify store had weak product pages with unconvincing copy, and their lead response system was non-existent — potential customers were falling through the cracks.

What We Did

  • Shopify Store Rebuild: Redesigned the entire store layout with professional product pages, compelling copy, clear CTAs, and optimized product descriptions.
  • Content Writing: Wrote high-converting product descriptions, category pages, and brand copy that spoke directly to their target audience.
  • Facebook Ads Overhaul: Fixed audience targeting, created ad creatives that actually resonated, and built a structured campaign that drove real traffic.
  • WhatsApp Response System: Set up a streamlined process for capturing and following up with leads via WhatsApp — their primary sales channel.

The Results

Within one month of relaunch, the results were dramatic:

Rs 265,180 Total Sales Revenue
128 Orders Placed
4,810+ Unique Website Visitors
4,990+ Total Sessions
864 Peak Daily Sessions
50+ Payments to Capture

What Made This Work

The turnaround came from fixing the fundamentals: a store that actually converted, ads that reached the right people, and a system that captured every lead. At 128 orders and Rs 265K in revenue from month one, Onezon Store went from struggling to scaling — proof that the right strategy transforms everything.
