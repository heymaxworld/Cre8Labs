5 Signs Your Landing Page Is Costing You Sales

📝

Your landing page is the most important page on your website. It's where clicks turn into customers — or where they bounce forever. Yet most service-based businesses have landing pages that are actively working against them.

Here are 5 signs your landing page needs an overhaul, and what to do about it.

1. Your Headline Doesn't Say Anything Specific

"Welcome to Our Website" or "We Provide Quality Services" are not headlines — they're filler. Your headline should clearly communicate the transformation your customer gets. If a visitor can't understand what you do in 3 seconds, they're gone.

Fix: Lead with the outcome. "Get More Patients in 30 Days" beats "Dental Marketing Services" every time.

2. No Clear Call to Action

If your landing page has multiple links, generic buttons like "Learn More", or no obvious next step, visitors will freeze. Decision paralysis is the enemy of conversion.

Fix: One primary CTA above the fold. Make it specific: "Book Your Free Audit" not "Submit".

3. You're Selling Features, Not Benefits

Nobody cares about your features. They care about what those features do for them. "24/7 support" is a feature. "Never lose another lead because you were asleep" is a benefit.

Fix: Rewrite every feature as a benefit. Start each one with "So you can..."

4. No Social Proof

A landing page without testimonials, case studies, or trust signals is a page that screams "untested." People need to see that others have succeeded with you before they'll take a risk.

Fix: Add at least 2-3 specific testimonials with names and results. Better yet, add a case study with real numbers.

5. It's Slow on Mobile

Over 70% of traffic is now mobile. If your landing page takes more than 3 seconds to load on a phone, you're losing more than half your potential conversions.

Fix: Compress images, remove unnecessary scripts, and test on real mobile devices — not just the browser inspector.
